# Table Features

Part of the [Word Poker rules](../FINAL_RULES.md).

---

# 35. Shuffle Button

- Unlimited uses per hand.
- Rearranges the display order of the player's tiles only.
- Helps the player see different word combinations.
- Does not change which tiles the player has.
- Leaves the tiles already in the word where they are: only the tiles the word does not use
  move.

# 36. Pre-Selected Actions

While waiting for their turn, a player may pre-select what to do when it comes:

- **Fold**
- **Check**
- **Call**
- **Raise** (at a chosen amount)

When the turn begins, the pre-selected action is taken at once, so a player who pre-selected
does not stall the table or run down their turn clock.

## When a Pre-Selection Is Cleared

A pre-selection is cleared, and the player acts normally, when it no longer fits the table:

- **Check** is cleared if a bet arrives before the player's turn.
- **Call** is for the amount shown when it was chosen. It is cleared if that amount changes.
- **Raise** is cleared if its amount is no longer a legal raise when the turn begins.
- **Fold** is never cleared.

Every pre-selection is also cleared at the end of each betting round. The player may change
or cancel a pre-selection at any time before their turn.

## Word Building

A player may pre-confirm their draft. When §19's window opens, the draft is confirmed at once
if it can be built from the player's tiles. Otherwise the pre-confirmation is dropped and the
player confirms by hand.

# 37. Anonymity

Tables are anonymous, like anonymous poker tables: during play, no player sees another
player's username or chosen avatar.

- Other seats appear under an anonymous seat label and a neutral avatar.
- The player's own seat appears as **You**.
- The same applies to nameplates, action messages, showdown results, and hand summaries.
- The table browser shows how many players are seated, never who.

This prevents players from targeting one another.

---

# 38. Chip and Winner Motion

## Floating Chips

- Every blind, bet, call, raise, and all-in sends that seat's chips floating toward the pot.
- At payout, each pot's chips float to the seat that won it.
- The motion keeps the pot readable in real time as the hand progresses.

## Winner Highlight

- The winning seat's avatar gets a celebratory highlight, such as a glow or spotlight.
- The effect plays in place. It never takes over the screen or leaves the table.
- The table stays visible, and the next hand can start right after it.

When the player has turned animations off (§39), chips and highlights appear in place
without motion.
