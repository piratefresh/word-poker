# 39. Accessibility

Word Poker must be playable with assistive technology and adjustable to the player's needs
(ADA). Every setting below is per player and applies at every table.

## Text-to-Speech

- Letter tiles are read aloud, including both faces of a choice tile and any multiplier.
- Submitted words are announced at the showdown.
- Every control and every piece of table state works with the platform's screen reader.
- The Word Checker announces the remaining-check message after each lookup (§43).

## Visual Settings

- **Bigger tiles**: a larger tile size option.
- **High contrast**: a high-contrast color mode.
- **Font size**: adjustable text size.

## Audio and Motion Settings

- **Reduce motion**: turns animations off. Chips, reveals, and highlights appear in place
  (§38).
- **Sound effects off**: turns game sound effects off.
- **Silent mode**: visual only. No sound of any kind.
