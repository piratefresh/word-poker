# Offline free play has real stakes: busted seats sit out, the session ends

The offline free-play table seeds a fixed 1,000-chip Table Stack per seat and — unlike
§27's wallet-funded re-buy, which belongs to the online/lobby epic — lets a seat that
reaches zero sit out for the rest of the session. The session ends when the hero busts
or fewer than two seats can play; "New game" restarts with fresh stacks and a fresh
seed. Auto-reseeding busted seats was rejected: free play without loss teaches no
betting discipline, and an end-state makes the chips mean something.

**Consequences**: hands play shorthanded as bots bust; the felt keeps busted seats
seated at $0 with a BUSTED pill rather than re-lying out the table.
