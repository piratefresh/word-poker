# Two-letter tiles are choice tiles only

The product spec contradicts itself: its section on two-letter tiles says the player chooses
ONE of the two letters, but its Phase 6 says two-letter tiles "must be used together (cannot
split)". The rules had kept both readings as two tile kinds — either/or choice tiles and
fixed-sequence tiles (TH, ER, IN, ED) that play both letters in order.

Every two-letter tile is now a choice tile, and fixed-sequence tiles are gone. The spec's
dedicated section is explicit and worked through, its word-validation rule caps words at 2–7
letters (which a sequence tile would break), and its example pairs are all choice pairs;
Phase 6's line reads as "a tile cannot be split into two played letters". The bag holds single
letters only and the deal places choice tiles from a configurable pair pool — the spec's
eleven pairs — at most three among any seat's seven tiles.
